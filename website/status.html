Understanding account status

Connected

Recent account information has been received. Check the displayed update time before relying on a value.

Delayed

The latest information may be older than expected. Treat it as a previous update, not a live quote.

Unavailable

No current account information is available. Check the broker connection and contact support if the issue continues.

Important: The dashboard is for account visibility and market context. It is not a trading terminal, does not provide personalized investment advice and cannot guarantee data availability or trading outcomes.
