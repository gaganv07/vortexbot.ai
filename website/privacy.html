Privacy and data governance

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information VORTEX AI uses to provide account access, trading visibility and customer support.

Your credentials matter: VORTEX AI does not ask for or need your broker trading password. Never share it through the website or support channels.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, VORTEX AI may process the following information:

  • Website account details: Email address, display name, account identifier, sign-in and access status, and permissions associated with your website account.
  • Connected account information: Broker account identifiers and server details, account currency, balance, equity, margin, account mode, open positions and trade-history information made available through your connected account.
  • Security and usage information: Login and access timestamps, browser and device details, network address and security events used to protect accounts and investigate service issues.
  • Support messages: Information you choose to send when contacting our support team.

2. Information we do not request

  • We do not ask you to enter your broker trading password or investor password into the VORTEX AI website.
  • We do not request your bank password, card PIN or one-time banking codes.
  • Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, private keys or other authentication secrets to customer support.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate approved users, display connected account information, show account and market status, protect the service, troubleshoot issues and respond to support requests. We do not use website account details to authorize trading activity.

4. Service providers

VORTEX AI relies on third-party service providers for website hosting, account authentication and secure data storage. These providers may process information on our behalf as required to operate the service. Access is limited to what is necessary for the relevant service function.

5. Data retention and requests

We retain account and service information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, protect account security, maintain relevant records and meet applicable obligations. You may contact support to request access to or deletion of information associated with your website account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

6. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and security checks intended to protect account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in method private and notify support if you suspect unauthorized access.

7. Updates and contact

This notice may be updated when the service or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact us through WhatsApp support or Instagram @vortexbot.ai.
