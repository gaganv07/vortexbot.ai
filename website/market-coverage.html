Broker compatibility is account-specific

A symbol appearing in a broker's MT5 terminal does not by itself establish compatibility. The exact broker server, account type, symbol suffix, contract specification, trading hours and available market history must be verified before the integration can be considered supported.

  • Confirm the exact symbol and any broker-specific suffix.
  • Check contract size, tick size, spread, margin, commission and trading schedule with the broker.
  • Verify that the account type and server provide the data required by the integration.
