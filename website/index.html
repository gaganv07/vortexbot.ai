How it works

From broker connection to clear visibility

The private dashboard separates broker-confirmed activity from system status and feed freshness.

01 / CONNECT

Connect your MT5 account

The trading terminal connects to the broker using the broker's own platform and credentials.

02 / VERIFY

Verify broker data

Account status, positions and trade history are read from broker-confirmed snapshots when the signed feed is available.

03 / MONITOR

View your dashboard

Approved users sign in to view read-only trading information and the last successful broker-update time.

Access is currently restricted to approved preview users. The website is not an order-entry terminal and does not offer public live-trading activation.
