Getting started

Start with a clear account view

Follow these steps to access your VORTEX AI dashboard and understand the information shown for your connected trading accounts.

01

Prepare your broker account

Make sure your broker account is active and connected through the broker’s own trading platform. Confirm that prices and account information are updating there.

02

Request website access

Use an authorized VORTEX AI account. If you do not yet have access, contact the support team through the official channels.

03

Sign in securely

Open the sign-in page and use an available authorized sign-in method. Website sign-in is separate from your broker account.

04

Review account information

Open the dashboard to review balance, equity, margin, account mode, open positions and trade history when fresh broker information is available.

05

Check freshness and context

Review each account’s connection state and last update time. Market information is provided for context only and may be delayed or incomplete.

06

Keep credentials private

Never enter your broker trading password, investor password or one-time sign-in code into the VORTEX AI website or send it to support.

Ready to open your dashboard?

Dashboard access is available to authorized users. Displayed account data depends on a valid connection and a recent broker update.

Important operating boundaries

  • The website provides read-only account visibility. It cannot place, modify or close trades.
  • Compatibility and data availability depend on your broker, account type and connected instruments.
  • Demo and live accounts must be clearly distinguished. Live trading involves risk of loss.
  • Never rely on delayed or unavailable account information as a current market quote.
