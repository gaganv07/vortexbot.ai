Econometric Terminology Reference Guide
ADX (Trend Strength): Measures trend momentum regardless of direction. Values >= 25 confirm directional trend persistence.
Choppiness Index (CHOP): Uses fractal dimension to determine if price action is directional (<38.2) or choppy/consolidating (>61.8).
Hurst Exponent (H): Quantifies autocorrelation. H > 0.55 indicates trending persistence; H < 0.45 indicates Mean Reverting (Sub-diffusive) dynamics; H ≈ 0.50 is a geometric random walk.
O-U Half-Life: Ornstein-Uhlenbeck parameter representing the expected bars for a mean-reverting price excursion to decay 50%.
Variance Ratio VR(k): Tests the random walk hypothesis. VR(k) < 1 implies negative serial correlation (mean-reverting regime).
