Frequently asked questions

Key details about the platform, supported instruments, MT5 connection and account funding.

What is the minimum starting deposit?

The stated starting trading-account capital is USD 200. This amount stays in your broker account. It is not a fee paid to VORTEX AI. Your broker may require a different minimum or impose instrument-specific margin requirements.

When does the bot's trading session begin?

The configured session opens at 12:30 PM India Standard Time (IST / UTC+05:30), equivalent to 07:00 UTC, on eligible weekdays. From this time, the bot evaluates potential entries. An order is only considered when strategy signals, risk controls, market availability, news filters and broker execution checks permit. The session opening does not guarantee a trade.

Does VORTEX AI guarantee returns?

No. Automated trading carries significant risk. Backtests, demo results and prior performance do not guarantee future results. You can lose some or all of the capital in a REAL trading account.

Does the website place or manage trades?

No. The VORTEX AI website dashboard is read-only. The separate MT5 bot may execute trades according to its deployed configuration; the website itself cannot place, change or close orders.

Do I need MetaTrader 5?

Yes. The trading system connects to a MetaTrader 5 terminal and a compatible broker account. Exact symbol names, suffixes, contract specifications and available market data vary by broker.

Which instruments are currently configured?

The current configured roster contains XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, NAS100, US30 and BTCUSD. Instrument availability depends on the broker and enabled bot configuration. XAGUSD is not included in the currently released roster.

What is the current product release status?

Limited access · integration testing. The current scope is a restricted, read-only dashboard preview for approved test accounts. VORTEX AI is not generally available, and public customer onboarding is closed. Broker compatibility and snapshot freshness must pass validation before any broader release.

Can every signed-in user see the shared MT5 feed?

The current Preview integration is designed to show the configured shared read-only account to valid signed-in users. The production website is not switched over, and the service is not generally available. A sign-in session never gives a user access to unshared accounts.

Is VORTEX AI open for public sign-up?

No. Access is currently limited to pre-approved accounts while the broker feed, production authorization and database security controls are being validated. Signing in with Google does not automatically authorize access to trading data.

Can anyone use email sign-in to create an account?

No. The email sign-in link is configured for existing accounts only; it does not automatically create new users. The dashboard also checks the signed-in email against a server-side allowlist before returning trading data.

Why might the dashboard show no trades?

The dashboard requires a connected MT5 publisher, server-side feed credentials, an authorized signed-in account and a recent verified snapshot. No sample trades are presented as verified broker data.

Should I share my MT5 password with support?

No. Never send your broker password, one-time code, private API key, HMAC secret or full account credentials. Support may ask for a redacted screenshot, broker/server name and the approximate time of an issue.
