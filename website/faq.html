Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers about account access, connected trading information, market context and the limits of the VORTEX AI website.

What is VORTEX AI?

VORTEX AI provides a central view of connected trading accounts. Authorized users can review account balances, margin information, open positions, completed trades, market context and account connection status.

What information appears in the dashboard?

The dashboard displays information received from connected broker accounts, including balance, equity, margin, account mode, open positions and trade history. Some market information may also be shown when the relevant data is available.

Does the VORTEX AI website place or close trades?

No. The website is read-only. It does not submit, change or close trading orders. It is designed to display account information and market context.

How does account information reach the dashboard?

Your trading account remains connected through your broker’s own trading platform. VORTEX AI receives account information through a separate connection and displays it after it has been checked. The website never needs your broker trading password.

Why does an account show delayed or unavailable information?

Account information may be delayed when the broker connection is interrupted, the trading platform is offline, or fresh data has not been received. Check the account status and last update time. Do not treat an old value as a live quote.

What is the difference between demo and live accounts?

The dashboard identifies the account mode reported by the broker. Demo activity uses simulated funds; a live account involves real capital and carries financial risk. Always check the account-mode label before interpreting any figures.

Which brokers and trading accounts are supported?

Compatibility depends on the broker, account type, available instruments and data connection. Support may vary. Contact VORTEX AI before relying on a broker connection that has not been confirmed for your account.

How do I access my dashboard?

Go to the sign-in page and use an authorized VORTEX AI account. Access is restricted to approved users. If your account has not been authorized, contact the support team.

Does VORTEX AI need my broker password?

No. Never enter or send your broker trading password, investor password, one-time sign-in code or private account credentials through the VORTEX AI website or support channels.

Does market intelligence provide trading advice?

No. Market information is provided for context and research only. It may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable for some instruments. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell and should not replace independent risk assessment.

How can I contact customer support?

Use our official WhatsApp support channel or Instagram account. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.
